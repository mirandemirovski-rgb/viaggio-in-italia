Il metodo

Un vero corso di italiano. Solo, diverso.

A real Italian course, told as a journey. Each city is a complete lesson unit, built the way serious language courses are built.

I livelli

The journey follows the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), the standard used across Europe to describe language skills. It goes from A1 (first contact) to A2 (elementary) and B1 (independent). The North of Italy is level A1.

Ogni città, una lezione

  • Clear goals: what you will be able to say and understand by the end.
  • Vocabulary in context: words you discover in the scene, with audio to hear how they sound.
  • Grammar step by step, in the order taught by the major coursebooks.
  • Dialogues, reading, listening and writing: all four skills.
  • Culture: what Italians say, do and eat, and why.
  • Exercises, and a final test before you can move on.

Le città-prova

Every few stops, a test city checks everything you have learned so far, like the review units of a coursebook.

Le fonti

  • The CEFR level descriptors of the Council of Europe.
  • The reference syllabi for Italian as a second language from the Italian certification bodies: CILS (Università per Stranieri di Siena), CELI (Università per Stranieri di Perugia), PLIDA (Società Dante Alighieri) and CERT.IT (Università Roma Tre).
  • The unit-by-unit progression of widely used coursebooks, such as Nuovo Espresso (ALMA Edizioni) and Nuovissimo Progetto italiano (Edilingua).
